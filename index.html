Why Velour

Where fabric meets mastery

Every decision — silk weight, drape angle, stitch tension — is made with one question: will she feel extraordinary in twenty years?

Lyon Silk

Sourced from the oldest silk weaving houses on Rue de la Soierie, Lyon. The weight, lustre, and drape are unlike any synthetic — they age with the grace of wine.

Hand Embroidery

Beaded detailing and gold threadwork applied entirely by hand. Each embellishment is placed by our senior ateliers — no two dresses are ever identical.

Parisian Construction

Internal boning, French seams, and hidden structure sewn to withstand a full evening of movement. The fit is architecturally precise — every dart is calculated.

We produce each collection in strictly limited runs. No warehouses, no excess — just 200 gowns per design, numbered and certified. When they're gone, they're gone.

How It's Made

The process

From raw silk to your silhouette — four stages, zero shortcuts.

01
Fabric Curation

Our team visits Lyon, Como, and Paris twice yearly to select silk, organza, and embroidered toile. Only 6% of bolts are accepted. Touch, drape, and sheen must all be flawless.

02
Pattern Cutting

Patterns are drafted on paper toile, then refined entirely by hand. Three fittings per silhouette. Curves that a block can rough — only a couturier's eye can perfect.

03
Hand Assembly

Every seam is sewn by a single master tailor from start to finish. Seven stages of internal pressing and finishing achieve a surface and fall that machine sewing simply cannot.

04
Final Fitting

Each gown is inspected against our fit standard. It is then numbered, certified on a woven label, and wrapped in acid-free tissue for delivery or in-atelier collection.
